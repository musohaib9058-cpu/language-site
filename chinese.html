Chinese (Mandarin) Lesson 1: Greetings and Basic Words

Chinese is written with characters. Each character is a word or part of a word. The "How to say it" column uses Pinyin, which writes Chinese sounds in English letters. The small marks above letters (like ǎ) show the tone: in Chinese, the same sound with a different tone can mean a different word. Press a Listen button to hear the tones.
