About this website

Language Learning for Everyone teaches beginner words in English, Hindi, Urdu, Chinese and Japanese. It is designed for everyone, including people who are blind, deaf or colour blind.

How this website is accessible

  • Everything works with a keyboard only (Tab, Enter, Space and arrow keys).
  • It works with screen readers: clear headings, named buttons and spoken messages.
  • Every word is marked with its language, so screen readers pronounce it correctly.
  • Everything you can hear is also written down, for deaf users.
  • Colour is never the only way information is shown, for colour-blind users.
  • The Settings page offers bigger text and a high-contrast mode.
  • Every page has the same layout and menu.

How this website was made

This website was made by Sohaib as a university project (ITNPBD4). The code and lesson content were created with help from an AI assistant. All Urdu and Hindi words were checked by a native speaker. Chinese and Japanese words were checked with online translation tools, so they may still contain mistakes.
