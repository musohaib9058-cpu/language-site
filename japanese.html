Japanese Lesson 1: Greetings and Basic Words

Japanese uses three writing systems together: hiragana (for Japanese words and grammar), katakana (mostly for foreign words and names) and kanji (characters that came from Chinese). The "How to say it" column uses Romaji, which writes Japanese sounds in English letters. Press a Listen button to hear the word.

Japanese greetings and basic words
Japanese How to say it (Romaji) English meaning Listen
こんにちは konnichiwa Hello
お元気ですか？ o-genki desu ka? How are you?
元気です genki desu I am fine
私の名前はソハイブです watashi no namae wa Sohaibu desu My name is Sohaib (the name is written in katakana)
ありがとう arigatō Thank you
お願いします onegai shimasu Please
はい hai Yes
いいえ iie No
水 mizu Water
さようなら sayōnara Goodbye
